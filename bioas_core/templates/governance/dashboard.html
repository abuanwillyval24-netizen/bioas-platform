14A. Philippine Statutory & Legal Policy Framework
Law / IssuanceOperational Rule Enforced in BIOAS
RA 7160 (Local Government Code)Governs Barangay executive/legislative powers, Katarungang Pambarangay mediation before court filing, and the 20% Barangay Development Plan (BDP).
RA 11261 (First-Time Jobseekers Act)Enforces a 100% fee waiver on Barangay Clearances and certifications for qualified first-time jobseekers with Oath of Undertaking.
RA 9262 (Anti-VAWC Act)Automatically masks victim and respondent identities (CONFIDENTIAL — RA 9262) on the dashboard while maintaining clearance holds.
RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act)Restricts 201 resident data access by LGU tier (Barangay full access vs. City/Province aggregated statistics only).
RA 11032 (Ease of Doing Business / ARTA)Tracks certificate issuance turnaround times and standardizes frontline service requirements.
RA 10121 (PDRRM Act)Tracks BDRRMC hazard alerts, evacuation center occupancy, and 70% Mitigation / 30% Quick Response Fund (QRF) COA liquidation.
